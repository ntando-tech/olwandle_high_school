Best School For Your Kids

Discover Olwandle High School, a dynamic hub of learning in the lively kwaGamalakhe Township since 1991! Our school day, buzzing from 07:30 to 14:30, promises an exciting journey for students in grades 8 through 12. What makes us stand out? Well, hold onto your hats – education here is absolutely free! Yes, you read that right – no school fees! And as a bonus, each learner receives complimentary stationery every year.

But there's more to the Olwandle experience. Our unique feed scheme ensures students from various backgrounds are ready to tackle academic challenges. Picture this: grades 8 to 12, a comprehensive educational haven for every student.

Now, let's talk about our extraordinary teaching team – 35 passionate educators who go beyond the norm. They're not just teachers; they're mentors, guides, and enthusiasts, ensuring learning is an adventure. Through regular workshops, they stay on top of schoolwork and bring innovative teaching methods to the table.

And the adventure doesn't stop when the bell rings at 14:30. Afternoon classes beckon, allowing eager minds to dive deeper into subjects, fueling a thirst for knowledge beyond regular hours.

Welcome to Our School Rules

Respect Peers

Treat everyone with kindness and understanding, creating a positive learning environment for all.

No Bullying Tolerated

Everyone has the right to feel safe and respected. Harassment and bullying will not be accepted.

Honesty Matters

Be truthful in your actions and words, building trust and respect within the school community.

Responsibility Counts

Complete your tasks on time, take ownership of your learning, and contribute to a positive classroom.

No Fighting

Cleanliness is Key
